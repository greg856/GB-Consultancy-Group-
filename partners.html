Strategic Partners

Advisory backed by proven delivery capability.

GB Consultancy Group maintains formal strategic partnerships with the organisations that turn our advisory work into built infrastructure.

Innovate StadiaUNITED KINGDOM · innovatestadia.co.uk

Innovate Stadia Limited is a UK-based specialist in modular stadium construction, structural composites, and BIM-based prefabrication, targeting UEFA and FIFA standards. GB Consultancy Group works alongside Innovate Stadia on advisory and project management engagements across the UK market.

Innovate Stadia SystemsPOLAND · innovatestadia-systems-pl.com

Innovate Stadia Systems Poland Sp. z o.o. is the Polish subsidiary of Innovate Stadia Limited, specialising in modular construction, structural composites, and BIM-based prefabrication for the Polish and wider Central European market.

Partnership Structure

How the relationship works.

A

Advisory, Not Ownership

GB Consultancy Group holds no equity stake in Innovate Stadia Limited or Innovate Stadia Systems Poland. The relationship is a formal advisory and coordination partnership.

B

Shared Standards

Both partners are held to the same UEFA/FIFA-aligned technical and safety standards that govern GB Consultancy Group's own advisory work.

C

Cross-Border Continuity

Because Innovate Stadia operates in both the UK and Poland, clients working across borders retain a single point of contact through GB Consultancy Group.
