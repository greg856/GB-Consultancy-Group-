About the Group

A decade of advisory, built for the demands of modern sport.

GB Consultancy Group (also trading as JB Consultancy Group) advises on the development of modern, multi-purpose sports facilities across the European Union, with a primary operational focus on Ireland, Poland, and the United Kingdom.

Our Origin

Formed to close the gap between design intent and delivery.

Sports infrastructure projects fail less often for lack of ambition than for lack of coordination — between architects, contractors, regulators, and financiers. GB Consultancy Group was formed to sit at that intersection: a single advisory relationship spanning feasibility, delivery oversight, and legal compliance.

Over more than ten years, that approach has been applied to modular stadium construction, structural composite systems, and BIM-based prefabrication projects built to UEFA and FIFA standards.

How We Work

One advisory relationship, three disciplines.

I

Independent, Not Integrated

We are not a construction firm and do not hold a financial interest in any contractor's output. Our advisory position remains independent of the delivery partners we coordinate with.

II

Structured Around the Full Lifecycle

Engagements typically begin at feasibility and continue through construction oversight and regulatory sign-off, rather than being scoped to a single phase.

III

Backed by Formal Partnerships

Delivery capability is extended through formal strategic partnerships with Innovate Stadia Limited (UK) and Innovate Stadia Systems Poland — see our Strategic Partners page.
