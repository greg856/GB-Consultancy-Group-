01 — Who We Are

Advisory built at the intersection of sport, structure, and statute.

GB Consultancy Group works alongside club owners, federations, developers, and public bodies to bring modern stadium and sports-facility projects from feasibility through delivery. We do not build; we advise, structure, and protect the interests of those who do.

Our work spans architectural feasibility, on-site project oversight, and the legal scaffolding that keeps a cross-border development compliant from planning through hand-over.
